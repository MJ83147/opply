The reality

SME accounts cost more to serve than they return

The revenue is there, but the economics are hard. Small F&B brands are expensive to acquire, slow to pay, and time-consuming to manage. Here's where most suppliers feel it.

Customer acquisition eats margin

Reaching SME buyers takes outreach, meetings, and onboarding time that enterprise accounts don't. The cost per customer is high, and the volumes don't always justify it.

High admin per small order

POs, invoices, delivery confirmations, payment chasing. Each small account generates as much paperwork as a large one, with a fraction of the revenue.

Slow and unpredictable payments

SME brands often extend payment terms as far as they can. Late payments and reconciliation disputes create cash flow pressure and admin overhead that compounds over a large customer base.

Patchy, hard-to-forecast volumes

Small brands order inconsistently, making it difficult to plan production runs and manage inventory. The aggregate demand is significant but it arrives in fragments.
