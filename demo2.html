The reality

Ops shouldn't be the thing that slows you down

The supply chain work that was manageable at launch gets heavier as you grow. Here's where we see brands losing the most time and money.

Deliveries you can't rely on

When your on time, in full rate hovers around 60%, every week is a gamble. Late ingredients mean missed production run deadlines, and retailers don't wait around.

Everything lives in spreadsheets

Ingredients, suppliers, POs, delivery dates. It works until it doesn't, and by then you've already missed a restock window.

Cash tied up in ingredients

You're paying suppliers before your customers have paid you. That gap gets wider as you scale, and it limits what you can say yes to.

Your week disappears into admin

Chasing supplier confirmations, reconciling invoices, placing reorders. The work that keeps the lights on is the same work keeping you from growing.
