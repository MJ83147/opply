How we calculated this

For each ops task, we multiply your weekly hours by Opply's automation rate for that task (based on your revenue tier: $1M to $2M). Total weekly hours saved is annualised (x 52 weeks) and multiplied by your hourly rate ($65,000 salary / 2,080 hours = $31/hr) to produce the annual value.
